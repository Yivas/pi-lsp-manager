# Code of Conduct

`pi-lsp-manager` welcomes respectful technical discussion, bug reports, security reports, and contributions.

## Expected behavior

- Stay on topic and provide enough technical detail to understand a claim.
- Treat people with respect when they disagree or make mistakes.
- Give constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Protect private information and use the security channel when disclosure could cause harm.
- Accept moderation decisions and stop an interaction when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, trolling, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or attempts to bypass moderation.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe in a professional project space.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, including public representation of the project.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy. Depending on severity and history, the maintainer may warn or restrict a participant.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action for abuse, harassment, or exposed private information. Do not post private evidence in an issue. GitHub documents this process in [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
